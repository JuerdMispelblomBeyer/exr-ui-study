# Dark guided quiz flow

## Reference identity and evidence quality

- Source: `reference/2127bf6c37eb2b2b36aebf8ce225b4d2.jpg`.
- Type: editorial product-flow composition containing three UI states.
- Evidence: high for composition and staged progression; medium for UI detail;
  low for actual motion.

## Executive design reading

The source turns a multi-step interaction into one continuous spatial story.
Connected panels make sequence visible before the user reads labels, while a
single warm signal unifies focus, progression, and final action.

## Dimension analysis

### QUIZ-AES-01 — Overall aesthetic and personality

**OBSERVATION** — Soft black panels, low-key product photography, and a glowing
warm path create a tactile, staged environment.

**INTERPRETATION** — The experience feels considered and premium because the
interaction is presented as a physical journey rather than a sequence of pages.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Use material depth and one spatial continuity cue
to make a consequential multi-step process feel coherent.

### QUIZ-COMP-01 — Composition, grid, and alignment

**OBSERVATION** — Three unequal panes alternate across a vertical axis and are
joined edge-to-edge by a routed line.

**INTERPRETATION** — Asymmetry supplies forward motion while connector endpoints
make parent/next-state ownership explicit.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — For guided diagnosis or recovery, compose states as
a connected path whose geometry reveals order without copying this zig-zag.

### QUIZ-DEN-01 — Density and whitespace

**OBSERVATION** — Each pane contains one question or outcome, surrounded by
generous black space.

**INTERPRETATION** — Low local density prevents the process from feeling like a
form and makes each decision consequential.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — In high-risk or infrequent flows, show one decision
per stage and use whitespace to isolate it from background complexity.

### QUIZ-TYPE-01 — Typography and hierarchy

**OBSERVATION** — Large plain-language headings dominate; uppercase step labels
and muted body text create secondary levels.

**INTERPRETATION** — The type ladder explains stage, task, then consequence with
minimal chrome.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Make the user's immediate decision the largest text,
with process metadata quiet but consistently placed.

### QUIZ-COLOR-01 — Color and contrast

**OBSERVATION** — Orange appears in the connector, selected option, step labels,
and final action; surfaces remain near-black.

**INTERPRETATION** — A single hue becomes both narrative thread and active-state
signal. Greyscale hierarchy still holds through scale and luminance.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Assign the lead accent to progression, selection,
and commitment, while keeping passive surfaces neutral.

### QUIZ-GEO-01 — Geometry, borders, shadows, surfaces

**OBSERVATION** — Broad rounded panes use faint borders and ambient shadows;
connector geometry is thin and exact.

**INTERPRETATION** — The contrast between soft containers and precise logic makes
the sequence approachable but controlled.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Contrast a forgiving outer surface language with
exact inner paths, controls, or diagrams.

### QUIZ-IMG-01 — Imagery and iconography

**OBSERVATION** — Abstract product material appears in the first pane; realistic
product photography completes the final pane; option icons are simple outlines.

**INTERPRETATION** — Imagery moves from atmosphere to proof as the flow resolves.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Let imagery become more concrete as a workflow moves
from orientation to outcome; use real system imagery rather than stock garnish.

### QUIZ-HIER-01 — Information hierarchy, interaction, motion

**OBSERVATION** — Selected radio state and CTA are visible; the connected layout
implies progression but no transition is shown.

**INTERPRETATION** — Interaction confidence is high for selection and continue,
low for motion. Spatial continuity suggests focus could travel along the path.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Preserve the visible origin of each next state and
use motion only to reinforce that continuity.

### QUIZ-DIST-01 — Distinctive decisions and limits

**OBSERVATION** — The connector is both navigation and composition; the final
product image breaks the text-only rhythm.

**INTERPRETATION** — The flow is memorable because relationship, depth, and
accent share one meaning.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Build signature from a recurring functional device,
not from unrelated glows or floating cards.

## Signature moves

| Move | Observation | Interpretation | Transfer | Do not copy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Routed luminous progression | One line physically joins every stage | Sequence is instantly legible | Connect diagnostic/recovery stages spatially | Exact route and orange glow |
| Alternating stage masses | Panels shift left/right | Asymmetry implies forward movement | Vary stage position around one stable axis | Source proportions |
| Material-to-proof imagery | Abstract form becomes product result | Outcome gains credibility | Increase imagery specificity toward resolution | Cosmetic products |
| One-decision panes | Each panel asks one thing | Reduces cognitive load | Isolate safety/recovery decisions | Marketing emptiness in routine UI |
| Accent continuity | Selection, path, and CTA share hue | Color has one coherent meaning | Let yellow bind current step and commitment | Exact palette/glow |

## Translation decisions

- **Borrow:** spatial sequence, one-decision focus, accent continuity, and
  increasing concreteness toward outcome.
- **Ignore:** browser frame, skincare copy, product arrangement, and vertical
  poster proportions.
- **Brand-specific:** warm cosmetic photography and orange material lighting.
- **Accidental:** staged composite does not prove responsive behavior.
- **Anti-patterns/limitations:** too spacious for routine monitoring; glass and
  glow would reduce operational contrast if applied everywhere; connector-only
  meaning needs labels and non-color cues.
