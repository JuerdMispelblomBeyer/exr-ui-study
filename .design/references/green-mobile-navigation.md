# Green mobile navigation — connected active ribbon

## Reference identity and evidence quality

- Source: `reference/d2ddc0f57326ea621f1119cb808ee518.jpg`.
- Type: mobile navigation state study.
- Evidence: high for geometry and selection continuity; low for content and desktop applicability.

## Executive design reading

The reference turns selected navigation into a continuous physical ribbon: the
white content surface bends into a deep-green rail around the active icon. The
move makes location tactile, but its mobile ornament should be translated with
restraint.

## Dimension analysis

### GN-AES-01 — Aesthetic and personality

**OBSERVATION** — A white content plane flows into a dark-green rail at the
selected icon.

**INTERPRETATION** — The active destination appears physically connected to the
content, not merely highlighted.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Make selection alter the relationship between
navigation and workbench, not just the icon color.

### GN-COMP-01 — Composition, grid, and alignment

**OBSERVATION** — Icons remain fixed on a vertical axis while the boundary shape
moves to the active row.

**INTERPRETATION** — Stable alignment plus one changing boundary supports rapid
location recognition.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Keep destinations stable and move a narrow ExR
yellow probe/connector to the active scope.

### GN-DEN-01 — Density and whitespace

**OBSERVATION** — The study contains little information beyond navigation state.

**INTERPRETATION** — Its spacing cannot be transferred directly to a dense
engineering interface.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Borrow the state cue only; size it to compact
desktop navigation and preserve workbench capacity.

### GN-TYPE-01 — Typography and hierarchy

**OBSERVATION** — Typography is absent or too limited to evaluate.

**INTERPRETATION** — No font, scale, or label behavior can be inferred.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Pair the pattern with persistent labels or
tooltips; do not derive typography from this reference.

### GN-COLOR-01 — Color and contrast

**OBSERVATION** — Deep green and white create a strong surface inversion.

**INTERPRETATION** — Selection is visible through luminance and geometry before
hue.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Let selected state remain legible in greyscale;
yellow should reinforce rather than carry it alone.

### GN-GEO-01 — Geometry, borders, shadows, and surfaces

**OBSERVATION** — Paired concave and convex curves form an organic notch around
the active icon; shadows are minimal.

**INTERPRETATION** — Custom geometry creates identity but is costly and can feel
playful if overused.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Translate the continuity into a restrained attached
lens, edge bridge, or contour—not a literal wavy notch everywhere.

### GN-IMG-01 — Imagery and iconography

**OBSERVATION** — Familiar monochrome icons sit at regular intervals.

**INTERPRETATION** — Icon-only navigation works for a small familiar set, not a
large technical taxonomy.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Limit icon-only treatment to stable global tools;
use text and node IDs deeper in the system.

### GN-HIER-01 — Information hierarchy, interaction, and motion

**OBSERVATION** — Multiple static frames imply the ribbon relocates on selection;
no transition timing is evidenced.

**INTERPRETATION** — Geometry supplies strong current-location feedback.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — If animated, move selection continuity briefly and
functionally; do not infer elastic motion from stills.

### GN-DIST-01 — Distinctive decisions and limits

**OBSERVATION** — The interlocking boundary is the only signature move.

**INTERPRETATION** — A product can gain recognition from one repeated state
transition, but the pattern is not a full visual system.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Own one ExR connection grammar across navigation,
topology selection, and inspectors.

## Signature moves

| Move | Why it works | Transfer | Do not copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interlocking active surface | Makes destination and content feel joined | Attached diagnostic lens | Literal mobile notch |
| Moving boundary | Strong current-location cue | Yellow probe edge tracks scope | Elastic animation assumption |
| Stable icon axis | Preserves muscle memory | Fixed global rail | Exact icons |
| Binary surface inversion | Survives greyscale | Neutral chassis/light lens | Green/white palette |
| State-specific custom geometry | Creates recognition efficiently | One ExR contour language | Use on every row/control |

## Translation decisions

- **Borrow:** structural selection, stable rail, attached-plane logic, greyscale
  legibility.
- **Ignore:** mobile proportions, sparse content, and literal curving cutout.
- **Brand-specific:** deep green palette and particular icon set.
- **Accidental:** the frames imply but do not prove motion.
- **Anti-patterns/limitations:** playful organic geometry can undermine technical
  precision; icon-only navigation is ambiguous; custom curves complicate dense
  states and accessibility.
