# Home energy — black and cream instrument grid

## Reference identity and evidence quality

- Source: `reference/fc14a5c09e381f8e860a9259563daf9b.jpg`.
- Type: direct energy dashboard concept.
- Evidence: high for surface hierarchy, chart craft, and image integration; medium for full navigation and interaction.

## Executive design reading

The design uses a hard black/cream modular grid rather than floating cards.
Large bar clusters and numeric ranges act as graphic objects, while a green-tint
room image provides a single atmospheric anchor.

## Dimension analysis

### HE-AES-01 — Aesthetic and personality

**OBSERVATION** — Hard black and cream planes alternate around bold bars,
numbers, and one tinted photograph.

**INTERPRETATION** — The UI feels architectural because surfaces are assembled
as one structure, not scattered as cards.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Treat the engineering viewport as a chassis with
joined regions and controlled inversion.

### HE-COMP-01 — Composition, grid, and alignment

**OBSERVATION** — Mixed-size modules share exact boundaries; the largest graphic
and image regions balance smaller readings.

**INTERPRETATION** — Rhythm comes from span and tonal contrast inside a strict
underlying lattice.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Use a stable grid but vary spans according to
overview, evidence, and action roles.

### HE-DEN-01 — Density and whitespace

**OBSERVATION** — Values and bars are large enough to read quickly, with ample
internal margin despite a filled viewport.

**INTERPRETATION** — Information density does not require tiny components.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Compress chrome and repetition before shrinking
critical data; preserve air around high-value readings.

### HE-TYPE-01 — Typography and hierarchy

**OBSERVATION** — Large numeric ranges act as compositional objects; labels are
small and aligned to module edges.

**INTERPRETATION** — Data scale establishes hierarchy more strongly than card
titles.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Let selected-system values, fault codes, and state
names occupy meaningful visual area; keep units and qualifiers compact.

### HE-COLOR-01 — Color and contrast

**OBSERVATION** — Black/cream inversion defines structure; green tint belongs to
the environmental image and limited highlights.

**INTERPRETATION** — Palette is role-based rather than distributed evenly.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Use neutral inversion for surface hierarchy and
reserve yellow for active signal/action, not whole decorative panels.

### HE-GEO-01 — Geometry, borders, shadows, and surfaces

**OBSERVATION** — Hairlines bind square modules into one grid; shadows and
floating elevation are absent.

**INTERPRETATION** — Contiguity makes the interface feel engineered and space
efficient.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Prefer joined workbench regions, inset instruments,
and shared dividers to a field of separated rounded cards.

### HE-IMG-01 — Imagery and iconography

**OBSERVATION** — A real room image is monochromatically tinted and cropped as
part of the grid.

**INTERPRETATION** — Photography supplies context and mood without introducing
an unrelated palette.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Integrate real robot/camera imagery in bounded
inspection regions and tone it only when state overlay remains readable.

### HE-HIER-01 — Information hierarchy, interaction, and motion

**OBSERVATION** — Large bars and numbers read first; smaller controls and labels
sit at edges. Static evidence does not reveal animation.

**INTERPRETATION** — Visual hierarchy is determined by consequence and scale.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Design the glance state first: platform status and
active fault must survive distance; interaction then reveals exact evidence.

### HE-DIST-01 — Distinctive decisions and limits

**OBSERVATION** — Joined high-contrast modules and oversized data marks provide
identity without gradient, glass, or ornamental icons.

**INTERPRETATION** — The system is memorable because composition and data are
graphic.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Pursue distinctiveness through chassis composition,
truthful instrumentation, and scale—not applied effects.

## Signature moves

| Move | Why it works | Transfer | Do not copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Joined inversion grid | Feels like one engineered object | Contiguous system/evidence chassis | Exact black/cream checkerboard |
| Oversized data marks | Improves glanceability | Dominant selected-state value/trace | Source numbers |
| Tinted real image | Adds context without palette noise | Camera feed or robot image | Green room treatment |
| Shared hairlines | Saves space and unifies surfaces | Workbench dividers | Exact line color |
| Span-driven rhythm | Avoids equal cards | Role-based module sizing | Exact module arrangement |

## Translation decisions

- **Borrow:** joined chassis, shared dividers, large truthful data, controlled
  inversion, integrated real imagery.
- **Ignore:** household energy content and exact checkerboard arrangement.
- **Brand-specific:** cream/green palette and source photography.
- **Accidental:** crop does not show full navigation or responsive behavior.
- **Anti-patterns/limitations:** too much inversion can fragment reading; large
  graphics may hide exact values; stark contrast can fatigue; every region must
  earn its span.
