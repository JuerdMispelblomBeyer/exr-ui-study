# Frosted workflow — layered application shell

## Reference identity and evidence quality

- Source: `reference/ref-005__light-frosted-workflow-dashboard__tall-navigation.jpg`.
- Type: close crop of a light workflow application.
- Evidence: high for navigation and material layering; medium for hierarchy; low for complete content and accessibility.

## Executive design reading

The screen creates depth through a black floating tool rail, a translucent
secondary navigation layer, and a pale work surface. The useful principle is
role-based material depth; the literal blur and low contrast need restraint.

## Dimension analysis

### FW-AES-01 — Aesthetic and personality

**OBSERVATION** — Light translucent panes overlap beneath a compact black
floating rail.

**INTERPRETATION** — The interface feels refined through material contrast, but
softness risks weakening operational clarity.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Use one dark, precise chassis layer against a warm
light workbench; keep translucent material limited to temporary context.

### FW-COMP-01 — Composition, grid, and alignment

**OBSERVATION** — Navigation depth is layered at the left while a broad content
surface remains open to the right.

**INTERPRETATION** — The shell distinguishes global tools from contextual
workflow without a top-heavy header.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Place compact global controls in a stable rail and
attach a contextual subsystem lens only when depth is needed.

### FW-DEN-01 — Density and whitespace

**OBSERVATION** — Large typography and broad transparent regions consume much
of the visible area.

**INTERPRETATION** — The image demonstrates atmosphere more than efficient
monitoring.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Borrow the layered shell but compress its spacing
for expert desktop use.

### FW-TYPE-01 — Typography and hierarchy

**OBSERVATION** — A large page word and large numeric value dominate; navigation
labels are compact and pale.

**INTERPRETATION** — Type creates strong scale contrast but may prioritize a
generic page title over system state.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Apply large scale only to selected robot/system
state, not “Dashboard,” and keep navigation labels readable.

### FW-COLOR-01 — Color and contrast

**OBSERVATION** — Black anchors a field of translucent white and grey; no strong
accent is visible.

**INTERPRETATION** — Tonal roles are clear at large scale but small controls
approach insufficient contrast.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Add ExR yellow as a precise active/probe mark while
meeting contrast requirements without relying on blur.

### FW-GEO-01 — Geometry, borders, shadows, and surfaces

**OBSERVATION** — Broad radii, frosted overlap, and soft shadows create multiple
floating planes.

**INTERPRETATION** — Material depth communicates role, but repeated rounded
glass can become generic and expensive.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Use translucency only for transient overlays or
attached inspectors; keep persistent diagnostic surfaces flat or inset.

### FW-IMG-01 — Imagery and iconography

**OBSERVATION** — The background is blurred into atmosphere and icons are small
monochrome utilities.

**INTERPRETATION** — Imagery is decorative rather than evidentiary.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Replace atmospheric blur with real camera/system
context; blur only when protecting readability over a live feed.

### FW-HIER-01 — Information hierarchy, interaction, and motion

**OBSERVATION** — The dark rail and frosted selected row imply navigation state;
no actual transitions are shown.

**INTERPRETATION** — Layer depth likely opens and closes with context, but that
behavior is not evidenced.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Make contextual panes clearly attached and
dismissible; avoid inventing slow glass animations.

### FW-DIST-01 — Distinctive decisions and limits

**OBSERVATION** — A black tool slab floats over a pale translucent nested shell.

**INTERPRETATION** — The material contrast is memorable, though familiar from
premium concept UI.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Translate the black-anchor/light-workbench contrast
into ExR’s instrument chassis, not a generic glassmorphic template.

## Signature moves

| Move | Why it works | Transfer | Do not copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dark floating tool rail | Creates a stable visual anchor | Compact ExR global rail | Exact capsule/slab |
| Layered navigation depth | Makes scope tangible | Temporary subsystem lens | Full-time translucent panes |
| Strong material contrast | Clarifies roles | Dark chassis, warm light workbench | Frosted aesthetic wholesale |
| Large orientation type | Gives immediate location | Selected system/fault identity | Generic “Dashboard” hero text |
| Soft focus background | Keeps overlay readable | Only over live imagery when necessary | Decorative blur |

## Translation decisions

- **Borrow:** material role contrast, compact global rail, attached context,
  selective overlay depth.
- **Ignore:** generic dashboard text, oversized spacing, decorative background.
- **Brand-specific:** exact glass recipe and source icon system.
- **Accidental:** crop and blur obscure actual content quality.
- **Anti-patterns/limitations:** low contrast; excessive rounded rectangles;
  glassmorphism can look generic; blur reduces precision and performance; the
  shown density is insufficient for an engineering overview.
