# Logistics tracking — coral matrix workbench

## Reference identity and evidence quality

- Source: `reference/f6c5437e7ada0bee51048397889b48b2.jpg`.
- Type: direct dark operational dashboard screenshot.
- Evidence: high for composition, matrix styling, and cross-view hierarchy; medium for interaction; low for motion.

## Executive design reading

The screen combines a bold editorial title with a dense operations workbench.
A coral status matrix becomes the memorable center while history, map, and table
provide alternate evidence without equalizing every module.

## Dimension analysis

### LT-AES-01 — Aesthetic and personality

**OBSERVATION** — Bold white type and a saturated coral matrix sit on a black
operations surface.

**INTERPRETATION** — The interface feels decisive because expressiveness is
concentrated in a useful instrument and its primary action.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Give ExR one recognizably branded diagnostic
instrument while keeping surrounding evidence disciplined.

### LT-COMP-01 — Composition, grid, and alignment

**OBSERVATION** — Modules use unequal spans: the matrix is prominent, while a
map, history, and table occupy supporting regions.

**INTERPRETATION** — The composition reflects the task rather than a reusable
four-card template.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Size topology, live traces, logs, and summaries by
decision value, not component uniformity.

### LT-DEN-01 — Density and whitespace

**OBSERVATION** — A relatively open title region gives way to compact evidence
with narrow gutters.

**INTERPRETATION** — Sparse-to-dense pacing prevents high information volume
from feeling immediate and flat.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Keep page orientation concise and allow the
workbench to carry dense, well-grouped telemetry.

### LT-TYPE-01 — Typography and hierarchy

**OBSERVATION** — The title is unusually large and tightly set; labels and values
are much smaller and regular.

**INTERPRETATION** — Type provides character, but the display scale may consume
too much space for everyday engineering use.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Use expressive scale on major system/fault identity
only, then switch quickly to compact interface and mono data roles.

### LT-COLOR-01 — Color and contrast

**OBSERVATION** — Coral appears in the heat matrix and CTA; most other content is
white or grey.

**INTERPRETATION** — One accent links status and action, but could conflate
semantics if every hot value shares it.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Keep ExR yellow for active/current/action and use
separate, named semantic colors for warning and danger.

### LT-GEO-01 — Geometry, borders, shadows, and surfaces

**OBSERVATION** — The shell is rectilinear and low-relief; rounded coral cells
give the matrix its own tactile language.

**INTERPRETATION** — Geometry differentiates evidence type, not every container.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Assign specific geometry to matrices, traces,
topology nodes, and controls rather than rounding all surfaces equally.

### LT-IMG-01 — Imagery and iconography

**OBSERVATION** — A map and matrix are the major visuals; icons summarize KPIs.

**INTERPRETATION** — Operational evidence replaces generic imagery.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Prefer system paths, timing matrices, and camera
frames over product renders inside the engineering application.

### LT-HIER-01 — Information hierarchy, interaction, and motion

**OBSERVATION** — Matrix cells, table rows, and map markers appear selectable;
the screen suggests cross-filtering but does not demonstrate it.

**INTERPRETATION** — Multiple evidence modes likely refer to the same tracked
objects.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Maintain selection identity across topology,
timeline, logs, and metrics; do not invent animation from the still.

### LT-DIST-01 — Distinctive decisions and limits

**OBSERVATION** — Editorial type, black field, and coral status matrix form a
coherent recognizable trio.

**INTERPRETATION** — Distinctiveness comes from coordinating orientation,
instrument, and accent.

**TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLE** — Build ExR recognition through a coherent trio:
platform silhouette, yellow probe path, and technical type/mark grammar.

## Signature moves

| Move | Why it works | Transfer | Do not copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accent status matrix | Creates a memorable evidence object | CAN message/rate or container health matrix | Coral cells |
| Unequal module spans | Reflects actual importance | Dominant topology, attached evidence | Exact layout |
| Editorial-to-technical type shift | Paces reading | System identity then compact telemetry | Exact font/scale |
| Cross-mode evidence | Supports triangulation | Diagram, traces, logs, table | Logistics content |
| Low-relief black shell | Keeps dense data coherent | Quiet dark diagnostic mode | Source palette |

## Translation decisions

- **Borrow:** signature matrix, asymmetric evidence grid, sparse-to-dense pacing,
  cross-view selection, low-relief shell.
- **Ignore:** shipment KPIs, map geography, and exact navigation.
- **Brand-specific:** coral palette, source typography, logistics vocabulary.
- **Accidental:** static image does not prove cross-filter behavior.
- **Anti-patterns/limitations:** oversized heading consumes capacity; accent may
  carry conflicting meanings; compact labels challenge readability; a matrix
  should not be used when topology or time is the actual question.
