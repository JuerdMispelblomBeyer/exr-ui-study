# V4 design review — Graphite Diagnostic Field

Date: 2026-08-22

## Direction

Visual thesis: one stable robot platform field exposes the causal path; a scarce yellow current probe and attached diagnostic lens reveal exact evidence without losing platform geography.

The v4 redesign preserves the v3 engineering architecture and adds three reference-derived principles without copying reference layouts:

- atmospheric light falloff behind the work surface;
- role-based depth, with persistent instruments inset and selected/temporary evidence raised;
- floating diagnostic plates only when they attach to a current path, selection, or consequential action.

Yellow means current selection, present sample, or decisive action. Orange means degraded. Red remains reserved for fault/stop semantics. Gradients describe material depth and optical focus; they do not encode health.

The interface passes the genericity test: removing logo, copy, and accent still leaves a recognizable platform topology, attached diagnostic lens, evidence dock, and Inspect → Diagnose → Change chassis. It does not reduce to a sidebar/KPI/chart SaaS template.

## Guardrails for later iterations

- Do not add floating tiles unless they express attachment, selection, temporary ownership, or consequence.
- Preserve stable compute/LAN/CAN geography across every lens.
- Keep the default screen glanceable at 1440 × 900; disclose deeper evidence rather than shrinking text.
- Keep yellow scarce. Never use a yellow surface wash as a generic brand treatment.
- Re-render the same viewport after every material composition change; a successful FigWright operation is not proof of visual quality.
