# Precision Platform Field v5 — visual review

Date: 2026-08-22  
Decision: **PASS**  
Reviewed from: rendered PNG output, not Figma layer structure alone

## Overall assessment

The restart establishes a recognizable engineering-interface point of view: a pale atmospheric workspace holds one dominant graphite causal field, a single raised diagnostic lens, and a joined evidence rail. The machine topology—not a grid of interchangeable cards—is the primary image. Yellow behaves as a live optical trace; orange identifies degradation; red remains reserved for stop or fault conditions.

The result meets the product brief at overview and drill-down levels. It presents computers, CAN-attached subsystems, networking, software runtimes, time synchronization, change review, and stale telemetry through one coherent spatial model.

## Strongest aspects

- The graphite platform field establishes an immediate focal mass and makes topology readable before details.
- The pale diagnostic lens creates depth, ownership, and a consistent place for the current question.
- Joined evidence replaces card soup with one continuous comparison surface.
- State changes preserve the mental model while changing the evidence: orange trace and probe, raised diagnostic plane, runtime ownership, failover path, clock uncertainty, controlled change review, and stale-node outline.
- Space Grotesk, Inter, and Roboto Mono have distinct editorial, interface, and machine-data roles.

## Highest-impact fixes completed

1. Replaced the rejected all-dark/card-category composition with a pale field and one dominant causal surface.
2. Made the system topology the primary visual object rather than a collection of KPI cards.
3. Introduced one floating diagnostic lens and limited depth effects to ownership-bearing surfaces.
4. Converted three evidence cards into a joined evidence rail.
5. Removed all collisions between large diagnostic values and their unit labels, then repaired the component master so future instances inherit the correct geometry.

## Genericity test

This cannot plausibly be mistaken for a generic SaaS dashboard, Tailwind template, AI landing page, or standard design-system demo. Removing the logo and copy still leaves a recognizable point of view: pale atmospheric workspace, graphite machine field, optical yellow trace, attached pale lens, and joined black evidence rail.

## Remaining refinement opportunities

- Add semantic component properties to reduce the small number of screen-level text overrides.
- Explore restrained transition prototypes for lens ownership and evidence-plane expansion.
- Test the smallest mono labels on the actual engineering display hardware before production handoff.

These are next-stage improvements, not blockers for the visual-direction pass.
